## Use each product section

Every product follows the same five-page contract:

1. **Overview** defines the product boundary, prerequisites, and authoritative sources.
2. **Use and configure** takes a site owner from setup to a verified outcome.
3. **Extend, hooks, and APIs** distinguishes supported integration surfaces from implementation details.
4. **Develop and test** identifies source ownership, build commands, tests, and contribution requirements.
5. **Troubleshoot and support** starts from symptoms, gathers safe evidence, and routes the problem correctly.

The machine-readable [product catalog](https://github.com/f/jetpack-docs/blob/main/docs/products/catalog.json) is the source of truth for product IDs, categories, ownership, public sources, and Jetpack monorepo paths. Product availability and commercial terms can change; confirm those details on the product's current public support page instead of treating this catalog as a pricing contract.
