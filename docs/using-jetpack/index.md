Jetpack is a family of WordPress products covering security, backups, performance, search, media, audience growth, and content creation. Begin with the outcome you need instead of enabling every feature at once.

## Recommended sequence

1. [Get started](get-started.md) and record the WordPress, PHP, and Jetpack product versions involved.
2. [Install, activate, and connect](install-activate-connect.md) one product at a time.
3. [Choose products](choose-products.md) according to a concrete site outcome.
4. Follow [common task routes](common-tasks.md) and the matching product guide.
5. Review [data, privacy, and consent](data-privacy-consent.md) before enabling a feature that sends or stores visitor data.
6. Keep [safe diagnostics](safe-diagnostics.md) ready before requesting support.
